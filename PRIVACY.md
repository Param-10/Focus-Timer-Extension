# Focus privacy

Last updated: 2026-09-05

Focus does not collect, store on a server, or transmit any personal data or browsing information. All data stays on your device.

## What stays on your device

Focus saves only what it needs to run the timer: focus and break lengths, whether the end chime is on, whether the next session should start automatically, and the current session (running, paused, or idle, and remaining time).

## How it is stored

That information is stored in this browser’s local extension storage. It is not synced to other devices and is not sent to Focus or to any third party.

## Third-party services

This extension does not use cookies, analytics, advertising, accounts, or third-party services. It does not read websites or browsing history.

## Data sharing

Data is not sold, shared, or used for anything other than running the timer you started.

## Retention and deletion

Data stays on this device until you change settings, reset a session, or remove the extension. Removing Focus clears its locally stored settings. A separate install in another browser starts with its own settings.

## Changes

If these practices change, this policy will be updated in the repository at the same URL.
